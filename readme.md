# NLP for Mental Health Call Centers in Uganda and Tanzania.

Developing speech recognition and NLP tools for mental health telephone services in Uganda and Tanzania, supporting English, Luganda, and Swahili.

## About

This project develops automatic speech recognition (ASR) and natural language processing (NLP) models and datasets for mental health in Uganda and Tanzania. We use call conversation recordings from hospital call centers at Butabika National Referral Mental Hospital (Uganda) and Mirembe National Mental Hospital (Tanzania) as primary data sources.

The project aims to provide insights into the role of communication quality in mental healthcare, how to assess it, and the feasibility of using digital health and AI technologies to improve mental health services. The datasets and models will also facilitate the advancement of NLP for African languages, which are under-resourced.

## Objectives

1. Explore the knowledge, attitudes, and practices of stakeholders in mental health regarding the use of ASR, NLP, AI, and other digital health tools
2. Curate and annotate speech datasets in English, Luganda, and Swahili
3. Develop ASR/NLP models for transcription, translation, sentiment and emotional analysis, topic modeling, and automatic communication quality assessment
4. Evaluate the developed models on prospective call center users and correlate communication quality with care process and outcomes

## Expected Outputs

- Labeled datasets for NLP in Luganda and Swahili for the mental health domain
- Open-source ASR and NLP models for topic modeling, emotion detection, speaker diarization, and conversation analysis
- Evidence on quality of communication and communication skills of healthcare providers in mental health
- Insights on the prevalence of different mental health conditions and information needs of patients derived from conversations and topic modeling
